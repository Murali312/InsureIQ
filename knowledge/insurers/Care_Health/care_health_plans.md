## 1. Product Name: Care Senior

- **Product Category:** Health Insurance Designed for Senior Citizens (Age 60+)
- **Product UIN:** `CHIHLIP21175V022021` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.careinsurance.com/health-insurance/care-senior-health-insurance
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.careinsurance.com

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Entry Age:** **60 years and above** (No maximum entry age limit; lifelong renewability).
- **Proposer:** Any adult individual with insurable interest; children can propose coverage for senior parents.
- **Pre-Policy Medical Checkup:** Required depending on age band and health history declarations.

### Coverage & Sum Insured Options:
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹3 Lakhs, ₹5 Lakhs, ₹7 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs.
- **Coverage Inclusions:** Pre-hospitalization (30 days), Post-hospitalization (60 days), Ambulance charges, Daycare procedures, AYUSH coverage, and Annual Health Checkup.

### Cost Sharing, Room Rent, and Sub-Limits:
- **Co-Payment:** **Mandatory 20% co-payment** per admissible claim. (Optional: Co-payment waiver rider can be purchased at higher premium).
- **Room Rent Limit:** Capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day; ICU capped at 2% of Sum Insured per day.
- **Specific Treatment Sub-Limits:** Sub-limits apply for cataract surgeries, joint replacements, and specified cardiovascular interventions.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days.
- **Named Ailments Waiting Period:** 24 months.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 24 to 36 months (depending on selected underwriting tier).

### Senior Citizen Suitability:
- Provides reliable coverage from a recognized standalone health insurer with optional riders to reduce co-payment burden.

## 2. Product Name: Care Supreme

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive High-Benefit Retail Health Insurance
- **Product UIN:** `CHIHLIP23018V012223`
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.careinsurance.com/health-insurance/care-supreme
- **Eligibility:** Age 18 to No Upper Limit. Dependent children 91 days to 24 years.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹7 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore.
- **Key Distinctive Benefits:**
  1. *Cumulative Bonus Super:* Accrues up to 500% increase on base Sum Insured over claim-free renewals.
  2. *Unlimited Automatic Recharge:* Full sum insured reinstates automatically every time it is depleted, for any illness in a separate hospitalization.
  3. *Room Rent:* **Zero Room Rent Capping**; Single Private AC room or any room category up to Sum Insured.
  4. *Co-Payment:* **0% mandatory co-payment**.
- **Waiting Periods:** 30 days initial; 24 months named ailments; 36 months pre-existing diseases (optional 1-year or 2-year reduction riders).
