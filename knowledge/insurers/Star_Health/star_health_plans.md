## 1. Product Name: Senior Citizens Red Carpet Health Insurance Policy

- **Product Category:** Specialized Health Insurance for Senior Citizens
- **Product UIN:** `SHAHLIP22028V062122` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.starhealth.in/health-insurance/senior-citizen-health-insurance
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.starhealth.in

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Entry Age:** **60 years to 75+ years** (Lifelong renewability guaranteed).
- **Medical Screening:** **No pre-policy medical checkup (PPMC) required** upfront; policy issued based on medical declarations and telephone verification.
- **Coverage Type:** Individual and Floater (Self and legally married spouse).

### Coverage & Sum Insured Options:
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹1 Lakh, ₹2 Lakhs, ₹3 Lakhs, ₹4 Lakhs, ₹5 Lakhs, ₹7.5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹20 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs.
- **Outpatient Consultations:** Covered up to specified limits at network hospitals.
- **Daycare Procedures:** All modern daycare procedures covered up to specified limits.

### Cost Sharing, Room Rent, and Sub-Limits:
- **Co-Payment:**
  - **30% mandatory co-payment** for all pre-existing disease claims.
  - **50% mandatory co-payment** for specified disease categories.
- **Room Rent Limit:** Capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day (Subject to proportionate deduction if exceeded).
- **ICU Charges:** Covered up to 2% of Sum Insured per day.
- **Specific Sub-Limits:** Sub-limits apply to cataract surgeries, joint replacements, and cardiovascular surgeries depending on the chosen Sum Insured.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** **Only 12 months (1 year)** for pre-existing diseases declared at proposal inception.

### Senior Citizen Suitability:
- Ideal for seniors who need fast coverage for existing conditions without undergoing pre-policy medical tests, provided the family accepts the 30%–50% co-payment structure and room rent sub-limit.

## 2. Product Name: Star Comprehensive Insurance Policy

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive Retail Individual and Family Floater Plan
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.starhealth.in/health-insurance/comprehensive-health-insurance
- **Eligibility:** 18 years to 65 years at entry; dependent children 91 days to 25 years. Lifelong renewability.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹7.5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹20 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore.
- **Room Rent Limit:** **No Room Rent Capping** (Single Private AC Room or actuals).
- **Co-Payment:** **Nil (0%)** for entry before age 60; 10% co-pay applies if entering after age 60.
- **Automatic Restoration:** 100% restoration of sum insured available up to 3 times in a policy year.
- **Maternity & Newborn Cover:** Covered after a 24-month waiting period.
- **Waiting Periods:** 30 days initial; 24 months for named ailments; 36 months for pre-existing conditions.
