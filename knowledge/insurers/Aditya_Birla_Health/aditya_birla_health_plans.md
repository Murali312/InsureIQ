## 1. Product Name: Activ Health Platinum Enhanced

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive Health Insurance with Chronic Disease Management
- **Product UIN:** `ADIHLIP21334V022021` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/healthinsurance/activ-health-platinum-enhanced
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.adityabirlacapital.com

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Adult Entry Age:** 18 years to No Upper Limit.
- **Dependent Child Entry Age:** 91 days to 25 years.
- **Relationships Covered:** Self, spouse, dependent children, dependent parents, parents-in-law.

### Distinctive Chronic Care Advantage:
- **Day-1 Coverage for Chronic Illnesses:** Under the *Chronic Management Program*, individuals with **Diabetes (Type-2), High Blood Pressure (Hypertension), High Cholesterol (Hyperlipidemia), or Asthma** receive coverage from **Day 1** for doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, and hospitalization related to these conditions, subject to enrollment in the wellness management regime.

### Coverage & Sum Insured Options:
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹2 Lakhs, ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore, ₹2 Crores.
- **Reload Benefit:** 100% reload of Sum Insured for unrelated and related illnesses.
- **HealthReturns™ Incentive:** Policyholders who maintain an active physical lifestyle (tracking steps via app) can earn **up to 100% cashback** of their annual premium.

### Cost Sharing & Room Rent:
- **Co-Payment:** **Nil (0% mandatory co-payment)** for standard age bands; voluntary co-pay available.
- **Room Rent Limit:** Any room category for Sum Insured of ₹10 Lakhs and above; Single Private AC room for ₹5 Lakhs.
- **ICU Charges:** Covered up to actual medical expense, no sub-limit.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days.
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period:** 24 months.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 36 months (reduced to Day-1 for declared chronic conditions under Chronic Management Program).

## 2. Product Name: Activ Care (Senior Citizens)

- **Product Category:** Dedicated Senior Citizen Health Plan (Ages 55+)
- **Eligibility:** 55 to 80 years at entry; lifelong renewability.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹3 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs.
- **Key Senior Benefits:** Dedicated Health Coach, Nursing Care at Home, Qualified Nurse allowance post-discharge, lifestyle support.
- **Cost Sharing:** Standard 20% co-payment (waived in higher tiers); sub-limits apply on cataract and knee surgeries.
