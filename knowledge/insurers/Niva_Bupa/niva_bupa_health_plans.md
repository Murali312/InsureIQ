## 1. Product Name: Senior First

- **Product Category:** Specialized Health Insurance for Senior Citizens (Age 60+)
- **Product UIN:** `MAXHLIP21096V012021` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.nivabupa.com/senior-citizen-health-insurance/senior-first.html
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.nivabupa.com

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Entry Age:** **60 years and above** (No maximum entry age limit; lifelong renewability guaranteed).
- **Proposer & Insured:** Individual and Floater (Senior self + senior spouse).
- **Underwriting:** Pre-policy medical screening based on age and declared medical history.

### Variants & Coverage Options:
- **Variants:** Gold and Platinum.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs.
- **Deductible Flexibility:** Offers voluntary deductible options (e.g., ₹25,000, ₹50,000, ₹1,00,000) that substantially decrease the annual premium payable.

### Cost Sharing, Room Rent, and Sub-Limits:
- **Co-Payment:** No mandatory co-payment on base claims if the selected deductible has been paid; zonal co-pay applies if treated outside your registered zone.
- **Room Rent Limit:**
  - *Gold Plan:* Single Private AC Room.
  - *Platinum Plan:* Any room category (including deluxe / suite up to Sum Insured).
- **Daycare Procedures:** All modern daycare procedures covered up to Sum Insured.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days.
- **Specific Ailment Waiting Period:** 24 months.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 24 to 36 months depending on plan tier.

### Senior Citizen Suitability:
- Flexible combination of high sum insured with deductible options, protecting elderly policyholders against high catastrophic bills while keeping annual premiums affordable.

## 2. Product Name: ReAssure 2.0

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive Multi-Benefit Retail Health Plan
- **Product UIN:** `MAXHLIP23023V012223`
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.nivabupa.com/health-insurance-plans/reassure-2-0.html
- **Eligibility:** Age 18 to 65+ (no upper cap under 2024 rules). Dependent children covered up to 25 years.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹20 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore.
- **Key Distinctive Benefits:**
  1. *AgeLock Feature:* Pay the premium applicable to your entry age until you make your very first claim.
  2. *ReAssure "Forever" Benefit:* Reinstates base cover unlimited times for any illness, related or unrelated, as soon as a claim is made.
  3. *Carry Forward (Lock the Bonus):* Unused base sum insured carries forward to subsequent years up to 5X to 10X of base cover.
  4. *Room Rent:* No room rent capping.
- **Waiting Periods:** 30 days initial; 24 months specific illnesses; 36 months pre-existing conditions (optional reduction riders).
