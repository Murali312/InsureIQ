## 1. Product Name: Optima Secure

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive Retail Individual and Family Floater Health Insurance
- **Product UIN:** `HDFHLIP23062V042223` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.hdfcergo.com/health-insurance/optima-secure
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.hdfcergo.com

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Adult Entry Age:** 18 years to No Upper Limit (following IRDAI 2024 Master Circular).
- **Dependent Child Entry Age:** 91 days to 25 years.
- **Relationships Covered:** Self, spouse, dependent children, dependent parents, parents-in-law.
- **Policy Tenures:** 1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years.

### Coverage & Sum Insured Options:
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹20 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore, ₹2 Crores.
- **Key Distinctive Benefits:**
  1. *Secure Benefit:* Instant 2X coverage from Day 1 at no additional premium (e.g., ₹10 Lakhs policy becomes ₹20 Lakhs coverage on Day 1).
  2. *Plus Benefit:* Coverage increases by 50% after Year 1, and 100% after Year 2 (up to 3X coverage).
  3. *Restore Benefit:* 100% automatic restoration of base sum insured if exhausted, available for any illness (related or unrelated).
  4. *Protect Benefit:* Zero deduction for 68+ non-medical consumables (gloves, syringes, PPE, belts).

### Cost Sharing & Room Rent:
- **Co-Payment:** **Nil (0% mandatory co-payment)** for standard plans.
- **Deductibles:** Nil on standard plan; voluntary deductible options available to reduce premium.
- **Room Rent Limit:** **No Room Rent Capping**; single private AC room or higher as per sum insured eligibility.
- **ICU Charges:** Covered up to actual medical necessity, no sub-limit.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days (except accidental hospitalization).
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period:** 24 months (cataract, hernia, joint replacements, stones).
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 36 months (3 years) as per standard terms (optional riders available to reduce to 2 or 1 year).

### Senior Citizen Suitability:
- Suitable for senior citizens seeking zero co-pay and no room rent cap; however, medical screening (PPMC) applies for seniors, and premiums reflect older age bands.

### Relevant Exclusions:
- Aesthetic/cosmetic surgery, self-inflicted injury, unproven/experimental treatments, treatment for substance abuse.

## 2. Product Name: my:health Medisure Classic

- **Product Category:** Basic to Mid-Tier Medical Insurance
- **Official Website:** https://www.hdfcergo.com
- **Eligibility:** 18 years and above; lifelong renewability.
- **Sum Insured:** ₹2 Lakhs, ₹3 Lakhs, ₹4 Lakhs, ₹5 Lakhs.
- **Room Rent Limit:** Capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day; ICU capped at 2% of SI per day.
- **Co-Payment:** 10% co-payment applies if admitted in a higher-tier city than registered, unless opted out.
- **Waiting Periods:** 30 days initial; 24 months for named ailments; 36 months for pre-existing conditions.
