## 1. Product Name: Complete Health Insurance (CHI) / Health AdvantEdge

- **Product Category:** Comprehensive Retail Individual and Family Floater Health Insurance
- **Product UIN:** `ICIHLIP21558V052021` (Verify latest schedule for active endorsement)
- **Official Product Page:** https://www.icicilombard.com/health-insurance
- **Customer Information Sheet (CIS):** Available at https://www.icicilombard.com

### Eligibility & Entry Conditions:
- **Adult Entry Age:** 18 years to No Upper Limit (following IRDAI 2024 Master Circular).
- **Dependent Child Entry Age:** 91 days to 25 years.
- **Relationships Covered:** Self, spouse, dependent children, dependent parents, parents-in-law.

### Coverage & Sum Insured Options:
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹15 Lakhs, ₹20 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore.
- **Key Distinctive Benefits:**
  1. *Reset Benefit:* Automatically restores 100% of base sum insured if exhausted in a policy year.
  2. *Donor Expenses:* Full organ donor hospitalization expenses covered up to Sum Insured.
  3. *Inpatient AYUSH:* Full coverage for Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy hospitalizations.
  4. *Domestic Road Ambulance & Air Ambulance:* Covered as per policy terms.

### Cost Sharing & Room Rent:
- **Co-Payment:** **Nil (0% mandatory co-payment)** for standard plans; optional deductible/co-pay riders to lower premium.
- **Room Rent Limit:** **No Room Rent Sub-Limit** for Sum Insured of ₹5 Lakhs and above; Single Private AC room or actuals.
- **ICU Charges:** Covered up to actual medical expense, no sub-limit.

### Waiting Periods:
- **Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days.
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period:** 24 months.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 24 to 36 months (optional rider for 1-year PED reduction).

## 2. Product Name: Golden Shield (Senior Citizen Focused Cover)

- **Product Category:** Dedicated Senior Citizen Health Insurance
- **Official Website:** https://www.icicilombard.com
- **Eligibility:** 60 years and above; lifelong renewability.
- **Sum Insured Options:** ₹3 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs.
- **Key Features:** Tailored healthcare concierge, home healthcare coverage, physiotherapy, and post-discharge rehabilitation.
- **Cost Sharing:** Co-payment options (10% to 20%) to keep premium affordable; sub-limits on specified modern treatments.
