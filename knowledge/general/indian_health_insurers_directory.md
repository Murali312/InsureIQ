## 4. Standard Regulatory Health Insurance Products Across All Insurers

By mandate of IRDAI, every general and standalone health insurer in India must offer standardized health insurance products with uniform terms, ensuring consumers can easily compare policies across different companies:

1. **Arogya Sanjeevani Policy:**
   - Standard basic indemnity health insurance policy offered by every insurer.
   - Uniform benefits: Sum Insured ₹50,000 to ₹10 Lakhs, standard 5% co-pay, standard room rent sub-limit (2% of SI or max ₹5,000/day, ICU 5% of SI or max ₹10,000/day).
   - Ideal for first-time buyers seeking basic, uniform medical protection.

2. **Saral Suraksha Bima:**
   - Standardized personal accident insurance policy covering accidental death, permanent total disability, and permanent partial disability.

3. **Corona Kavach / Corona Rakshak:**
   - Standardized Covid-19 health insurance covers launched during the pandemic.
