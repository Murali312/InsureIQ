# Health Insurer Customer Care, Toll-Free Helplines, and Grievance Escalation Directory

This directory provides verified customer care contact details, toll-free helpline numbers, and official grievance escalation channels for health insurance policyholders in India.

---

## 1. Official IRDAI Regulatory and Grievance Portals

When an inquiry is complex, unresolved by the insurer, or requires formal dispute resolution:
- **Bima Bharosa (IRDAI Centralized Grievance Portal):**
  - **Toll-Free Helpline:** `155255` or `1800 4254 732` (Available 24/7 across India)
  - **Email:** `complaints@irdai.gov.in`
  - **Website:** https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/
- **Insurance Ombudsman:**
  - Independent statutory forum for dispute resolution regarding claim repudiations, delays, or premium disputes up to ₹50 Lakhs.
  - 17 territorial centers (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune, Chandigarh, Kochi, etc.).
  - Process is free of cost for individual policyholders.

## 3. When Policyholders Should Contact Customer Care Directly

Policyholders are strongly advised to contact their insurer's customer care or TPA desk for:
1. **Active Hospitalization Emergency / Pre-Authorization:** Immediate cashless hospital desk coordination.
2. **Individual Claim Status Updates:** Real-time claim approval tracking requiring policyholder authentication.
3. **Policy Servicing & Endorsements:** Adding a spouse/newborn, changing address, updating nominees, or correcting name spellings.
4. **Portability Requests:** Must be initiated with the new insurer at least 45 days before policy expiry.
