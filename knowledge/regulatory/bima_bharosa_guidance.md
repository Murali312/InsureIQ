## 1. What is Bima Bharosa (formerly IGMS)?
Bima Bharosa is IRDAI's centralized online grievance management system. 
It provides an integrated platform for policyholders across India to register, escalate, track, and monitor consumer complaints against insurers and insurance intermediaries.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Grievance Escalation Process
1. **Level 1 - Approach the Insurer:**
   - Policyholders must first lodge their complaint with the insurer's Customer Care or designated Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO).
   - Insurers are mandated to acknowledge grievances within 3 business days and resolve them within **15 calendar days**.

2. **Level 2 - Register on Bima Bharosa:**
   - If the insurer does not resolve the complaint within 15 days or gives an unsatisfactory response, the policyholder can register the grievance on Bima Bharosa (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/) or call toll-free helpline **155255** / **1800 4254 732**.
   - A unique Token / Registration Number is issued to track the complaint status online.

3. **Level 3 - Insurance Ombudsman:**
   - If the dispute involves claim settlement delays, repudiations, premium disputes, or mis-selling, and remains unresolved for 30 days after approaching the insurer, the policyholder may approach the Insurance Ombudsman having territorial jurisdiction.
   - The Ombudsman process is free of cost for consumers, and awards up to ₹50 Lakhs can be granted.
