## 🖼️ Recommended Application Screenshots

When capturing updated screenshots from a running instance (`http://localhost:8501`), follow these standardized dimensions and states:

### 1. Main Consultation Interface (`insureiq-home.png`)
* **URL/State:** Initial load with empty message history.
* **Key Elements Visible:** Vibrant blue sidebar with Policybazaar styling (`#0077D9`), hero white *➕ New Chat* button, 4 navigation buttons, 6 welcome starter prompt cards, and the light sky blue composer bar (`#DFEFF7`).
* **Recommended Resolution:** 1280 × 800 px.

### 2. Conversational Q&A with Citations (`insureiq-chat-qa.png`)
* **Prompt to run:** *"What conditions do I need to meet to apply for health insurance in India?"*
* **Key Elements Visible:** User message pill, assistant avatar, grounded answer with IRDAI regulatory highlights, intent badge (`Category: General`), timestamp, and expandable citation card showing publisher, section, and clickable source URL.

### 3. Light Sky Blue Chat Input Composer (`insureiq-input-composer.png`)
* **Key Elements Visible:** Close-up of the bottom input field with light blue canvas (`#DFEFF7`), neutral border (`#D4E2EA`), placeholder text (*"Ask InsureIQ about coverage, health plans, waiting periods, or claims…"*) and borderless send button with vibrant blue `#0077D9` send arrow.

### 4. Compare Plans View (`insureiq-compare-plans.png`)
* **Navigation:** Click **⚖️ Compare Plans** in the sidebar.
* **Key Elements Visible:** Insurer multi-select filter, side-by-side comparison matrix across the 6 leading insurers, and deep-dive plan cards with verified filing badges.

### 5. Senior Care Hub (`insureiq-senior-care.png`)
* **Navigation:** Click **👵 Senior Care** in the sidebar.
* **Key Elements Visible:** 2024 IRDAI reforms banner (removal of entry age caps), 10-point senior evaluation checklist, and senior-specific policy comparison table.

### 6. Coverage Guide (`insureiq-coverage-guide.png`)
* **Navigation:** Click **📖 Coverage Guide** in the sidebar.
* **Key Elements Visible:** Financial insurance terms explained (co-pay vs deductible, room-rent sub-limits, proportionate deductions, and 36-month PED caps).

### 7. Claims & Support Hub (`insureiq-claims-support.png`)
* **Navigation:** Click **🛡️ Claims & Support** in the sidebar.
* **Key Elements Visible:** Cashless vs reimbursement steps, 3-tier statutory grievance escalation workflow (GRO $\rightarrow$ Bima Bharosa $\rightarrow$ Ombudsman), and customer care helpline directory.

### 8. Dark Mode Interface (`insureiq-dark-mode.png`)
* **Theme:** Browser or OS configured in Dark Mode.
* **Key Elements Visible:** Dark background with high-contrast text, uniform light sky blue composer bar (`#DFEFF7`), and blue send arrow.
