# General health insurance FAQs — India
These are general educational explanations, not a substitute for a specific policy contract or legal advice. If an answer depends on a product, jurisdiction, current regulation or personal claim, retrieve the applicable official source or ask a clarifying question.

1. **What is health insurance?** A contract that provides specified health-related benefits or pays/reimburses eligible medical expenses according to its terms.
2. **What is a premium?** The amount paid for insurance coverage for a stated period.
3. **What is sum insured?** A coverage limit specified by the policy, subject to terms, exclusions, sub-limits and other conditions.
4. **What is a waiting period?** A period during which specified benefits or conditions are not covered, as stated in the policy and applicable rules.
5. **What is a pre-existing disease?** Use the definition in the applicable policy and current rules; do not decide status from a short chat alone.
6. **What is cashless treatment?** A process where eligible, authorized expenses may be settled with a network provider, subject to policy terms and authorization.
7. **What is reimbursement?** A process in which the insured pays expenses and submits a claim for assessment under the policy.
8. **What is a network hospital?** A provider in the insurer/administrator's network; status can change and must be checked against a current source.
9. **Does cashless mean all expenses are covered?** No. Policy limits, exclusions, co-payments, deductibles and authorization conditions may apply.
10. **What is a deductible?** An amount the insured must bear before specified benefits apply, as defined by the policy.
11. **What is a co-payment?** A specified share of an admissible claim amount that the insured bears, as defined by the policy.
12. **What is a sub-limit?** A maximum payable amount for a particular benefit or expense category.
13. **What documents are commonly requested for claims?** Depending on claim type, examples can include claim forms, discharge summary, itemized bills, receipts and relevant medical records; use the insurer's current checklist.
14. **How long does claim processing take?** It depends on applicable rules, claim type, documents and review. Verify current official timelines; do not invent a guarantee.
15. **Can a claim be declined?** The insurer assesses the claim under the policy and applicable rules; refer to the formal decision and reasons rather than predicting an outcome.
16. **What should I do if a claim is denied?** Review the written reason and policy wording, contact the insurer's grievance officer, and use current official escalation guidance if unresolved.
17. **What is a family floater?** A policy design where covered family members share a sum insured, subject to the particular product terms.
18. **Can I renew my policy?** Renewal conditions and timelines depend on the policy and applicable rules; check the policy schedule and official insurer notice.
19. **Does every policy cover every treatment?** No. Coverage depends on policy wording, exclusions, limits, waiting periods, medical circumstances and applicable authorization.
20. **What is pre-authorization?** A request for review before a proposed treatment or settlement; it should not be represented as a guarantee that all final expenses will be paid.
21. **How do I complain about an insurer?** First check the insurer's current grievance process, then consult official IRDAI/Bima Bharosa guidance if needed.
22. **Where can I verify whether an insurer is registered?** Use the current official IRDAI insurer directory.
23. **Can I use one insurer's policy terms to answer for another insurer?** No. Retrieve the exact insurer/product/version wording.
24. **Can the chatbot tell me my claim status from a policy PDF?** No. Claim status requires an authenticated, current insurer system/API or a clearly labelled simulation.
25. **Can the chatbot guarantee my treatment will be covered?** No. It can explain retrieved wording and relevant uncertainties; binding decisions must come from the insurer's authorized process.
